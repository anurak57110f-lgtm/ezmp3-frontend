Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about using EzMP3.

1. Is EzMP3 completely free to use?
Yes! EzMP3 is 100% free to use. There are no hidden fees, subscriptions, or required registrations to convert and download your files.
2. Do I need to install any software or extensions?
No installation is required. Everything works directly in your web browser across all devices, including Windows, macOS, Android, and iOS.
3. What audio and video formats are supported?
Currently, we support high-quality MP3 for audio extraction and MP4 for video downloads.
4. Where are my downloaded files saved?
All files are automatically saved to your browser's default download folder (usually the "Downloads" folder on your device).
5. Is it safe to use this service?
Absolutely. We do not store any personal data or logs of your converted media links, ensuring a secure and private browsing experience.
